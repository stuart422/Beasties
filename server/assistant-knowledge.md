## How to: sign in

1. Open the Beasties website.
2. Type your email and password, then tap Sign in.
3. The very first time, you use a temporary password from Stuart. The site then asks you to choose your own password (at least 12 characters, typed twice).
- Forgot your password? Ask Stuart. He can give you a new temporary password.

## How to: change your password

1. Tap Account at the top right.
2. Tap My profile.
3. Under Change password, type your current password, then the new one twice (at least 12 characters).
4. Tap Change password. You stay signed in here; other devices are signed out.

## How to: browse the albums

1. Tap Photos in the top menu.
2. Tap an album name to open it.
- The Home page also shows the Fall 2026 photos, the next gathering and the album list.

## How to: see just the photos (full screen)

1. Open an album and tap any photo.
2. The photo fills the screen. Swipe, or tap the big arrows on the left and right, to move between photos. On a computer, the arrow keys work too.
3. Tap Close at the top right (or press Escape) to go back.
- Tap Details to see the caption, the day, Download and Comments. Tap Hide details to put them away.
- Full screen: the Full screen button hides the browser bars too, where the device allows it (iPad and computers; not iPhone).

## How to: see big photos with their captions and comments

1. Open an album.
2. Tap Big photos at the top (next to Grid).
3. Scroll down. Each photo is shown large, with its caption, the day, who shared it and the latest comments underneath.
- Tap Grid to go back to small photos. Your device remembers your choice.

## How to: show photos from one day

- In an album, tap a day button above the photos: All days, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, or Day not set.

## How to: add photos

1. Open the album.
2. Tap Add photos, then Choose photos.
3. Pick up to 20 photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP or iPhone HEIC; each under 30 MB).
4. You can type a caption for each one, then tap Upload.
- Location data is removed from every photo automatically.

## How to: write or edit a caption

1. Open the photo, then tap Details.
2. Tap Add a caption (or Edit caption).
3. Type up to 200 characters, then tap Save caption.
- Made a mistake? Tap Undo last change to bring back the previous caption.
- Who can edit: the person who added the photo can edit its caption. Stuart and the admins can edit any caption.

## How to: comment on a photo

1. Open the photo and tap Details (or, in Big photos, tap Add a comment under the photo).
2. Type your comment (up to 280 characters) and tap Post comment.
- You can delete your own comments. Stuart and the admins can delete any comment, or close comments on a photo.

## How to: download a photo

1. Open the photo and tap Details.
2. Tap Download.

## Random Beastie Sightings

- A gallery of its own for meetups and chance encounters between weekends, separate from the event albums.
- Find it in the Account menu (Random Beastie Sightings) or at the end of the Photos page.
- Tap Share a sighting, choose one photo, add the place and a short caption (up to 200 characters), then tap Share.
- You can edit or delete your own sightings. Stuart and the admins can edit or delete any.

## My photos and My profile

- Account > My photos shows the photos you added.
- Account > My profile lets you change the name shown with your photos and comments, and your password.

## Sign out

1. Tap Account at the top right.
2. Tap Sign out.

## The Members page (Stuart and admins only)

- Account > Members.
- Used to add a new member, reset a forgotten password, or remove someone. The site never sends email: it shows a one-time temporary password for Stuart to pass on.
- Only Stuart can make someone an admin.
- Stuart and the admins can also arrange the order of photos in an album (Arrange photos button in the album).

## Easier viewing

- Reduce motion: a switch at the bottom of every page and in the Account menu turns off moving effects.
- Text and buttons are large on purpose, and the site works on phones, iPads and computers.
