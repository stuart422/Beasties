A Family of Friends

We began as MeetUp friends and acquaintances who loved the outdoors.

Then came our first Foliage Weekend at The Beast in October 2024. What we thought might be a one-time gathering became the beginning of something truly special.

With every ride, every hike, every shared family-style meal and story, our friendships grew stronger. We showed up for the adventures and discovered something lasting.

Our friendships didn't stop at The Beast. They grew through trips, open homes, and ordinary days together.

Along the way, we became what Teri so perfectly called us, a “Family of Friends.”

It's the laughter, the conversations, the little moments, and the way we support and show up for one another that make our friendships special.

We welcome new faces, look out for one another, celebrate life's joys, and make memories that last long after the adventures end.

That's what it means to be a Beastie.

A private home for our photos, our plans and the weekends we share.
